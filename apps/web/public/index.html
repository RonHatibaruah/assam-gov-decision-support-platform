Platform Tutorial & Guide

What is this Application?

This system provides End-to-End Cryptographic Traceability across the Assam Tea Value Chain for the NESFIC-I-08 Challenge (TRA Tocklai). It connects 100,000+ Small Tea Growers (STGs) directly to Bought Leaf Factories (BLFs), NABL chemical residue testing labs, Guwahati Tea Auction Centre (GTAC), and global consumers.

🛡️ Assam GI Tag Protection 🧪 FSSAI & EU MRL Pesticide Compliance ⚖️ Bluetooth Digital Weighbridge 🔗 Polygon Layer 2 Merkle Anchoring

How to Use Each Portal:

🍵
1. Consumer QR Explorer (Leaf-to-Cup Verification)
For global buyers, retail consumers, and export inspectors
  • Step 1: Select any of the 6 quick demo chips or enter a custom QR token.
  • Step 2: View the verified Assam GI Authenticity Seal and TRA Tocklai Certification.
  • Step 3: Explore the interactive chronological timeline from harvest GPS in Jorhat to retail packaging.
  • Step 4: Click "Export Official Provenance Certificate" to generate a print/PDF certificate.
  • Step 5: Test the flagged non-compliant batch (ASM-2026-FLG-006) to see automated chemical safety interception.
🌿
2. Small Tea Grower (STG) / Field App (চাহ-সেৱা)
For smallholders, pluckers, and field collection agents
  • Language Switch: Click "অসমীয়া / EN" to switch between Assamese and English.
  • Geo-Fencing: Captures garden GPS coordinates; validates that plucking occurs strictly within registered Assam estate boundaries.
  • Bluetooth Weighbridge: Click "Sync Weighbridge Receipt" to simulate automated digital scale ingestion.
  • PPC Chemical Search: Search pesticides under the Tea Board Plant Protection Code (PPC v16) for safety advisory.
🏭
3. Estate & Bought Leaf Factory (BLF) Management
For factory supervisors, quality managers, and lab technicians
  • IoT Telemetry: Monitor live withering trough loss, fermentation humidity, and VFBD drying temperature curves.
  • Grading: Select processed tea grade (CTC BOP/BP/OF, Orthodox TGFOP1, or White Tea) and record recovery yields.
  • Lab Attestation: Issue NABL MRL pesticide compliance certificates with cryptographic IPFS pinning.
🏛️
4. TRA Tocklai Regulatory Command Center & Admin
For TRA scientists, Tea Board officers, and Govt of Assam regulators
  • Valley Heatmap: Click any pin on the Assam map to inspect regional smallholder and estate metrics.
  • GI Authority Manager: Authorize, suspend, or revoke Assam GI Tag certification for any batch with real-time audit logs.
  • MRL Alert Feed: Intercept and quarantine batches failing pesticide thresholds.
  • Merkle Root Anchoring: Aggregate batches and dispatch cryptographic state roots to Layer 2 ledger.

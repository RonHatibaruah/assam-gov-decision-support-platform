Platform Tutorial: What It Does & How to Use It

What is this Application?

This system is the official prototype built for the NESFIC-D-36 Challenge: "Trusted Government Knowledge, Rules & Document Assistant", organized by the Assam Innovation & Startup Foundation (AISF), DIIS, Govt. of Assam and engineered by Cool Minds Lab (Lead Applicant: Sambit Mohanty).

🏛️ Assam Administrative Staff College (AASC) 📜 Service Rules (FR & SR) ⚖️ Assam Pension Rules 1969 📝 Secretariat Note-Sheet Drafter 🛰️ ASSAC Drone & GIS Protocols 🛡️ 100% Zero-Hallucination Guardrails

How to Use Each Module:

🏛️
1. Rules & Knowledge Assistant (Query & Citations)
For civil servants, branch officers, and AASC trainees
  • Step 1: Select a quick prompt chip (e.g. "Pension Qualifying Service" or "Superannuation Age").
  • Step 2: View the authoritative answer accompanied by the Verifiable Citation Docket on the right.
  • Step 3: Click "Test Hallucination Guardrail" to test a fictitious query and watch the system safely refuse without inventing facts.
✍️
2. Government Note-Sheet & OM Drafter
For Section Officers, Superintendents, and Under Secretaries
  • Step 1: Select Document Type (Secretariat Note-Sheet, OM, or RTI reply).
  • Step 2: Enter subject line and specific facts.
  • Step 3: Click "Generate Official Draft" to see formatted government paper with 2.5-inch margins for officer remarks.
  • Step 4: Click "Print / Save as PDF" to generate the official print document.
🛡️
3. Admin Control Center & Audit Trails
For AASC Faculty, Department Heads, and IT Administrators
  • Step 1: Ingest new circulars using the Document Ingestion & Cryptographic Indexer.
  • Step 2: Inspect real-time officer queries and groundedness scores in the Audit Stream.
  • Step 3: Run the RAG Triad Automated Evaluation Suite to benchmark precision and citation coverage.

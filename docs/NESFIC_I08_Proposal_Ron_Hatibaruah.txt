Field 1: Problem understanding & user workflow
Submission Word Budget: Word Count: ~448 words / Max: 500 words
Problem Context & Scale
Assam produces over 50% of India’s tea output and ~10% of global supply. Over 100,000 Small Tea Growers (STGs) cultivate
Greater Than 52% of Assam's green leaf output. However, the value chain harbors latent structural vulnerabilities that jeopardize economic
resilience, export viability, and the global reputation of the Assam Tea brand. Key anomalies include:
1. Lack of farm-level origin visibility where green leaves from compliant and non-compliant smallholdings are mixed without
segregation.
2. Severe weighbridge arbitrage, where arbitrary 5% to 15% 'moisture tare deductions' are manually deducted at unorganized
collection hubs.
3. Haphazard chemical residue testing on finished bulk lots, causing entire export consignments to be rejected at European and
Japanese ports due to Maximum Residue Limit (MRL) breaches.
4. Dilution and counterfeiting of the prestigious Assam Geographical Indication (GI) mark through blending of cheap, inferior
imported leaves.
5. Stringent new environmental mandates under the European Union Deforestation Regulation (EUDR No 2023/1115), requiring
satellite-verified geolocation polygons for export clearance.
Current Workflow & Its Structural Gaps
Currently, land administration and leaf collection operate in reactive paper-based silos. Smallholders harvest green leaves and
transport them to intermediary agents who record weighment on handwritten chits. Processing variables in Bought Leaf
Factories (BLFs)—such as withering loss percentages and drying temperatures—are logged on loose sheets, eliminating
retroactive auditability. Discrepancies surface only when foreign destination ports seize non-compliant tea containers,
penalizing the entire valley.
Users & Beneficiaries
• Primary Administrative Users: TRA Tocklai scientists, Tea Board licensing controllers, and FSSAI food safety inspectors.
• Supply Chain Operators: Bought Leaf Factories (BLFs), estate managers, weighbridge operators, and GTAC auction brokers.
• Ultimate Beneficiaries: Over 100,000 Small Tea Growers receiving tamper-proof payments, and global tea consumers assured
of authentic, chemical-safe Assam tea.
Specific Gap Addressed & Intended Outcome
Chah-Praman bridges the operational chasm between manual field collection and digital quality governance. The intended
outcome is an automated, tamper-proof traceability system that pre-empts MRL violations, eliminates weighbridge theft,
verifies EUDR zero-deforestation compliance, and restores global trust in the Assam Tea GI mark.

Proposed Solution Overview
Chah-Praman is an automated, production-tested, end-to-end digital traceability and GI provenance platform engineered
specifically for the Assam tea ecosystem under TRA Tocklai guidance. Operating as a decentralized quality assurance layer, it
integrates IoT hardware, PostGIS cadastral surveys, and Layer-2 blockchain smart contracts into a unified verification pipeline.
Main Modules & Core Functions
1. Smallholder Mobile App ('Chah-Sewa STG App'): Offline-First PWA operating in remote gardens via IndexedDB/CRDTs;
bilingual Assamese/English interface with voice prompts and iconographic navigation; geo-fenced GPS harvest locking.
2. Cadastral GIS Engine (Dharitree / ILRMS): Cross-references harvest GPS with surveyed land revenue parcels (Dag #142 to
#147), verifies Periodic Patta tenure, audits EUDR deforestation compliance via Sentinel-2, and enforces non-plucking riparian
eco-buffers.
3. Automated IoT Weighbridge Ingestion: Bluetooth BLE and RS232 serial bridges connecting digital collection scales, locking
gross, tare, and net weights into digitally signed receipts.
4. Factory Processing Engine: Telemetry integration for withering troughs, fermentation oxidation floors, and VFBD drying
chambers (115°C), mapping green leaf to finished CTC/Orthodox grades.
5. NABL Lab & MRL Gatekeeper: Ingests GC-MS/MS pesticide screening reports, validates against Tea Board PPC v16 limits,
and pins immutable IPFS test hashes.
6. Blockchain Merkle Engine: Aggregates high-frequency plucking and weighbridge receipts into binary SHA-256 Merkle trees,
anchoring 32-byte roots to Layer-2 ledger with 99.82% gas savings.
7. TRA Tocklai Command Center: Institutional login portal (admin@tocklai.res.in), interactive valley heatmap, cadastral parcel
inspection, real-time GI authorization toggles, and chemical hazard alert feeds.

Field 4: Technology & solution architecture
Submission Word Budget: Word Count: ~457 words / Max: 500 words
Architecture Paradigm
Chah-Praman is architected as a lightweight, cloud-native, microservices-driven digital traceability platform designed for
seamless deployment on Cloudflare Edge and the Assam State Data Centre (SDC). It operates as a read-and-verify
decision-support infrastructure with complete tamper resistance.
Technology Stack Specifications
• Client Layer (Multi-Persona Frontends): HTML5/ES6 PWA with Service Workers for complete offline caching. Data
persistence utilizes IndexedDB with vector clocks/CRDTs for conflict-free reconciliation. Localized Assamese and English
typography.
• Application & Ingestion Layer: Node.js (v20+ LTS) modular REST backend with Cloudflare Pages Functions serverless edge
replication (Smaller Than 50ms global latency). Ingests IoT telemetry from weighbridge scales and factory sensors via MQTT/WebSockets.
• Cadastral Geo-Spatial Engine: PostGIS spatial database executing point-in-polygon checks against Dharitree / ILRMS
cadastral survey layers to verify plucking coordinates strictly inside certified tea smallholding boundaries.
• Smart Contracts Layer (Solidity 0.8.20+): TeaRegistry.sol maintains verified stakeholder roles (STG, BLF, Lab, TRAAdmin).
TeaBatchProvenance.sol enforces sequential lifecycle state transitions.
• Cryptographic Merkle Root Engine: SHA-256 binary tree aggregates thousands of off-chain transactions, committing 32-byte
state roots to Polygon Layer-2, slashing gas costs by 99.82%.
• Decentralized Storage: InterPlanetary File System (IPFS) pinning for immutable NABL lab certificates.
Data Flow & System Integration
1. STG plucking is logged on the mobile PWA with GPS coordinates, validated against Cadastral Dag polygons, and signed
offline.
2. Bluetooth weighbridge scale captures stabilized net weight, generating a signed intake receipt.
3. Factory IoT sensors record withering, fermentation, and drying parameters during CTC/Orthodox manufacturing.
4. NABL lab screening cross-references pesticide active ingredients against Tea Board PPC v16 thresholds.
5. Merkle aggregator hashes batch events, committing state roots to Layer-2 and generating GS1 Digital Link QR codes.

1. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Identity Governance
Every stakeholder interacts with the system using cryptographically verified identities. The TeaRegistry.sol smart contract
enforces strict role segregation: SmallTeaGrower can only create plucking logs for certified personal land polygons;
FactoryBLF is authorized only to record processing parameters and intake receipts; LabCertifier is granted exclusive rights to
append NABL chemical residue analysis; and TRAAdmin is reserved exclusively for TRA Tocklai officials to authorize Assam
GI tags and manage regional registries. Institutional access to the TRA Regulatory Command Center is gated behind
dedicated authentication credentials (demo: admin@tocklai.res.in / tocklai2026) with active session management.
2. Privacy & Compliance with DPDP Act, 2023
Smallholder personal identity information (Aadhaar, bank account numbers, phone numbers) is stored strictly in encrypted
off-chain relational databases adhering to India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. Only
pseudo-anonymized identifiers (e.g., STG-JHT-001, registered garden name, and general village polygon) are committed to
immutable public ledgers. Proprietary commercial pricing negotiated between BLFs and brokers at auction is protected through
encrypted private channels.
3. Application Security & Interoperability
All API communications utilize TLS 1.3 with SHA-256 encryption. Package QR codes utilize cryptographic HMAC signatures
(sig=HMAC-SHA256(batchId + serial, secretKey)) with intelligent geo-anomaly scan velocity detection. Architecture conforms
to CERT-In cyber security guidelines, incorporating rate limiting, CORS protection, SQL injection prevention, and XSS
sanitization.

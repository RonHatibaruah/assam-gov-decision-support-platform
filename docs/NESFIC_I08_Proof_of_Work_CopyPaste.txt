1. Executive Summary & Prototype Status
As mandated by the Assam Startup NESFIC 2026 Uniform Solution Proposal Requirements (USPR), a proposal narrative
alone is insufficient. This document provides empirical Proof of Work (PoW), architectural validation, wireframes, and test
benchmark results demonstrating a fully operational, high-fidelity software prototype of Chah-Praman.
The prototype successfully demonstrates:
1. Automated ingestion and dual-system reconciliation between textual revenue Jamabandi (Dharitree) and spatial cadastral
maps.
2. Deterministic execution of statutory agrochemical audit rules under the Tea Board Plant Protection Code (PPC v16).
3. Sub-second derivation of the composite Leaf-to-Cup Provenance Score and automated chemical hazard quarantine.
4. Interactive Cadastral WebGIS overlay featuring dynamic polygon styling, Dag & Patta tenure verification, and EUDR
anti-deforestation audit badges.
5. Mobile-first field harvest logging queue for Small Tea Growers (Chah-Sewa) with GPS coordinate lock and ground
measurement logging.
6. Citizen and consumer-facing verification portal generating downloadable, printable official Certificates of Provenance.
7. Super Admin Regulatory Command Console (admin@tocklai.res.in) enabling real-time calibration of statutory rule engine
tolerances, GI certification authorization/revocation, dual-system ingestion telemetry, and immutable SHA-256 compliance logs.
2. Live System Architecture & Technical Pipeline
The system operates on an enterprise cloud-native architecture deployed to Cloudflare Pages edge network with Layer-2
blockchain anchoring.
